Larkspur Montessori School
Tuition and Financial Aid Policy 2027-2028

Tuition
Larkspur Montessori is a nonprofit school. Tuition for the 2027-28 school year is $18,500 for the full day program (8:30-3:30). Aftercare until 5:30 is available for an additional $350/month. A materials fee of $400 is due at enrollment.

Financial Aid
Larkspur is committed to diversity and we offer flexible tuition. Financial aid is available for qualifying families. Families who would like to be considered for financial aid should let us know when they apply. Awards are based on need and on the funds we have available. Full pay families help make it possible for us to offer scholarships to families who need them.

Payment
Tuition may be paid annually, by semester, or in 10 monthly payments. A non-refundable deposit of $1,000 is due with the signed enrollment contract and will be applied to tuition. Tuition is due on the 1st of the month. A late fee of $50 applies after the 10th. Tuition is not refundable if a child withdraws during the year. Tuition is tax deductible as a childcare expense.

Siblings receive a 10% discount.

Questions? Email us at hello@larkspurmontessori.org.
