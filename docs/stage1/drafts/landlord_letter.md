Subject: Space inquiry for a new Montessori school

Dear Mr. Alvarez,

My name is Priya Raman and I am an Emerging Teacher Leader with Wildflower Schools. I am writing because I am in the SSJ and my Ops Guide suggested I reach out to you about the storefront at 412 Broadway.

I believe deeply that every child deserves an authentic Montessori education. Dr. Maria Montessori discovered over a century ago that children are naturally curious and that when we prepare the environment carefully and follow the child, they teach themselves. In a Montessori classroom, children choose their own work, move freely, and develop concentration, independence and a love of learning that lasts a lifetime. Our classrooms are peaceful, beautiful and filled with natural materials. We are also committed to anti-bias and anti-racist practices and to the liberation of every human being.

Wildflower is a network of teacher-led, shopfront Montessori micro-schools. Wildflower provides us with a Sunlight Loan and will guarantee our lease so you don't need to worry about the rent. We hope to open in the fall.

I would love to open Marigold Montessori in your space. It is a beautiful location and I walk by it all the time. We would need to make some changes for licensing but I'm sure we can work that out.

Please let me know if you are interested. I am so excited about this opportunity and I truly believe Marigold Montessori will be a wonderful addition to the community!!

Warmly,
